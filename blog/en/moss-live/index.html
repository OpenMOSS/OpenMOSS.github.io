MOSS-Live: Full-Duplex Interaction Compared with GPT-Live and Doubao

Twelve metrics reveal where real-time interaction improves—and where task performance still differs.

Real conversations include pauses, brief acknowledgments, changes of plan, and voices that are not addressed to the assistant. A system participating in that conversation has to decide when to keep speaking, when to wait, and when to yield the floor.

MOSS-Live puts these interaction decisions at the center of its design. In the evaluation charts published by MOSS, it leads all six reported full-duplex metrics across the compared models. Against GPT-Live-1 and Doubao SeedDuplex, the largest gaps concern irrelevant speech and interruption handling. Tool use and multi-turn task completion show a more mixed picture.

False responses to irrelevant speech show the largest separation: MOSS-Live scores 2%, which is 66 percentage points below GPT-Live-1 and 73 points below Doubao. Its premature-response rate during pauses is lower by 8.93 and 12.85 points, respectively. These metrics address two distinct failures: speaking before the user has finished thinking, and responding to speech intended for someone else.

Interference during a response is another test. MOSS-Live rejects 68% of irrelevant interruptions, compared with 21% for GPT-Live-1 and 13% for Doubao. Its robustness to backchannels reaches 98.17%. Together, these results suggest better control of conversational timing in the evaluated settings: preserving a response through an acknowledgment or unrelated sound while remaining receptive to meaningful input.

MOSS-Live scores 4.88/5 for response quality after interruption and 3.63/5 for entity tracking, correction, and safety, leading all models in the source chart on both measures. Alongside the interference results, these scores point to conversation continuity as a particular strength.

There are also clear limits. Its Big-Bench Audio accuracy is 88.40%, below Doubao at 90.50%. Its multi-turn instruction-following score is 3.35/5, below GPT-Live-1 at 3.72/5 and Doubao at 3.44/5. The supported claim is therefore an advantage in full-duplex interaction and conversational continuity, rather than an overall lead across voice capabilities.

Keeping conversation and background work moving

MOSS-Live supports full-duplex speech, semantic interruption, live video and screen sharing, and streaming simultaneous interpretation. Its architecture separates immediate interaction from longer-running work: a foreground model handles ongoing perception and conversation, a background model handles reasoning and tools, and a coordination layer shares memory, context, and task state.

For example, a user planning a trip can ask for a hotel search and then continue discussing itinerary preferences. The search proceeds in the background while the conversation continues; its result returns when ready. This design makes waiting time available for further discussion and lets new requirements accompany an evolving task.

Reading the results in context

For applications concerned with false responses in shared spaces, interference while speaking, and recovery after interruption, MOSS-Live deserves close evaluation. For complex multi-turn task completion, GPT-Live-1’s τ-Voice lead remains relevant. For audio-understanding accuracy, Doubao’s Big-Bench Audio advantage also matters.
